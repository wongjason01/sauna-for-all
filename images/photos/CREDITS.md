## sauna-for-all-2026-14.jpg
Caption: In 2020, UNESCO inscribed Sauna culture in Finland on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, recognizing sauna as a living cultural tradition passed between generations and shared in both homes and public places. The Global Public Sauna-Bathing Charter builds on this broader conversation about how sauna culture is shared, practiced, and carried forward as it grows internationally.
