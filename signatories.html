Sign the Charter, and meet those who already have

Sauna for All is built by the people and organizations shaping public sauna today — operators, governments, designers, researchers, industry partners, and bathers. Sign the Charter to add your name, or explore who has already committed.

Image: sauna bucket / detail photography
Signing is an application process — it asks for real commitments and takes about 30 minutes.

Sign the Charter

By signing, you commit to the Charter’s principles and to demonstrating how they are reflected in your decisions, operations, and stewardship.

  • Part A — Commitment: who you are, why you’re signing, your principles and commitments.

  • Part B — Contribution: stewardship, the sector, research, looking forward.

  • Part C — Consent and affirmation.

  • About 25–30 minutes.

  • Responses go to the founding stewards for review.

Jessica Rastas
Canada

“We are working to open a community sauna social enterprise in Kelowna, BC by 2027, with sliding-scale pricing built into the model from the beginning. We will use a portion of the funds generated by our floating sauna to help make this project possible and keep access affordable. We will also work with local community and health organizations to explore social prescribing and other ways of creating funded or lower-cost access for people who may not otherwise be able to afford sauna. As we develop the space, we will continue learning from and working with the communities around us so that cultural safety, accessibility and community needs are actually part of how the sauna is built and operated not something added on afterward.”
