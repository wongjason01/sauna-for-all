Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about signing, the Charter’s scope, and governance.

What is the Public Sauna-Bathing Charter?

A shared set of principles and norms for the responsible development and stewardship of public sauna — a common point of reference for communities, practitioners, operators, governments, funders, researchers, and industry. Read the full Charter →.

Who can sign the Charter?

Founding stewards, advisors and stewards, operators, governments and public bodies, designers, builders and developers, industry partners, and bathers and community members — see the full list on the Charter page →.

Is there a cost to sign?

No. Signing is free. It is, however, a genuine application — it asks for real commitments, not just a signature.

What happens after I apply?

Your responses go to the founding stewards for review. Once confirmed, you’re listed among signatories and welcomed into network channels.

Is the Charter a certification or standard?

No. It does not certify or rank public saunas, and it isn’t a technical standard or rulebook. It sets out shared principles, not specifications — see what the Charter is / isn’t →.

How can I get involved if I’m not ready to sign?

You can contribute time, funding, expertise, or space — see Support our work →, or stay close to the movement through the newsletter on our homepage.

Who governs and funds the Charter?

Sauna for All is entirely volunteer-led by an international steering group of founding stewards. See Founding Stewards →.
